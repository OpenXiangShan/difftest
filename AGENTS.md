# Difftest Working Guidelines

Before working under `difftest/`, determine whether the task is a potentially complex, multi-file change or requires iterative testing/debugging. If so, review the relevant files in `difftest/docs/` as needed before proceeding.

For complex tasks, use the planning and progress workflow in [`difftest/docs/workflow.md`](docs/workflow.md) when it helps coordinate multi-file work or preserve debugging context. Plan/progress notes are optional; do not create or commit `.github/*-plan.md` or `.github/*-progress.md` solely to satisfy this guideline. Put notes in the issue/PR or another suitable location when that is more useful.
